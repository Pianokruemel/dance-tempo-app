DanceTempo is an open-source companion app for ballroom and social dancers.

Tap along to any song to detect its tempo (BPM) and instantly discover matching Ballroom, Latin, and Social dances.

Key Features:
• Precise BPM tap engine with median outlier filtering
• Dedicated First-Beat (Downbeat) toggle to detect 3/4 waltz vs. 4/4 meter
• Color-coded tempo scale: Ideal dance-school speed, slow, or fast
• Explanations for excluded dances (meter mismatch, too slow/fast)
• Local song library with multi-dance annotation
• Automatic song metadata detection from Spotify, YouTube Music, and Deezer
• One-tap instant playback in your preferred streaming app
• Full JSON backup and restore via Android Storage Access Framework
• 100% free open-source software (GPLv3), privacy-friendly, no ads, no trackers
